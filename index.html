Questions

Who is SitterLark for?

SitterLark is built for two-parent households who already have trusted babysitters — and want a better way to manage them together. It’s not for finding new sitters; it’s for managing the ones you have.

Do both parents need to download the app?

Yes — and that’s the point. Once both parents are in, everything you do (add a sitter, book an event, log a payment) instantly syncs to your partner. One parent can use it, but two makes it click.

What if I only have one sitter?

The free plan covers up to 2 sitters and includes booking, scheduling, and real-time sync — no payment required. Most households start free and upgrade when they add a third sitter.

Is it just for babysitters?

Primarily, yes. SitterLark is optimized for babysitter and childcare management. Many families also use it for au pairs and regular nannies — anyone they book and pay on a recurring basis.

How does payment tracking work?

SitterLark tracks hours worked and hourly rate for each sitter, calculating what you owe at any time. When you’re ready to pay, it opens Venmo with the amount and recipient pre-filled. We don’t process payments ourselves — that happens in Venmo.

Is SitterLark available on Android?

Currently iOS only. Android is on the roadmap — download for iOS now and we’ll notify you when the Android version launches.

Is my household’s data private?

Yes. Your data is only visible to members of your household. We use Supabase for secure, encrypted storage and never sell your data.
