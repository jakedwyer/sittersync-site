How It Works

Everything in one
shared place.

Shared Sitter List

Add your babysitters once — both parents see names, numbers, hourly rates, and notes. No more hunting through texts or asking your partner for a contact.

Easy Booking

Create a childcare event, assign a sitter, and your partner sees it the moment it's booked. No more double-booking or discovering plans your partner already made.

Google Calendar Sync

Connect your Google Calendar and babysitting events sync automatically. Both parents stay aligned on the family schedule — no manual entry needed. (Premium)

Payment Tracking

Know exactly what you owe every sitter. Pay via Venmo in one tap — the amount and recipient are pre-filled. Full history, visible to both parents. (Premium)
