Frequently Asked Questions

How do I invite my partner?

Go to Settings and share your household invite code. Your partner enters this code during signup to join your household.

How do I connect Google Calendar?

Open Settings and tap "Connect Google Calendar." Sign in with your Google account and SitterSync will sync your babysitting events automatically.

How do payments work?

SitterSync tracks what you owe each sitter. When you're ready to pay, tap the Venmo button and the amount and recipient are pre-filled. SitterSync does not process payments directly.

What's included in Premium?

Premium gives you unlimited sitters, Google Calendar sync, full payment tracking with Venmo integration, and priority support. Free accounts are limited to 2 sitters.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. Go to your iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > SitterSync to cancel or change your plan.

How do I delete my account?

Email support@sittersync.app with your account email and we will delete all your data within 30 days.

Bug Reports
